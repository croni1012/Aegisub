# Hide all subtitle lines or masks only

[New features](README.md) · [Magyar](../hu/hide-subtitles.md)

**Where to find it:** Video context menu and toolbar.

Hide every line or only `\p1` mask lines in the video without changing their comment state. Opacity can also be adjusted.

## Commands for shortcut settings

- `video/toggle_mask`
- `video/toggle_subtitle`
