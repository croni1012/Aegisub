# AI scene review and full post-check

[New features](README.md) · [Magyar](../hu/ai-review.md)

**Where to find it:** AI → AI review / AI post-check.

With a valid OpenAI connection, you can review a short scene in context or step through suggestions for the complete subtitle file.

## OpenAI for text features

The OpenAI connection powers AI review and AI post-check.

### AI review – scene-based

- AI review examines a scene up to two minutes long.
- Feedback is based on the Japanese audio and the source line.

### AI post-check

- Use it for a textual review of the complete subtitle.
- Suggestions can be accepted or skipped individually.

**Important:** always check AI suggestions before accepting them.

A configured OpenAI connection is required. Costs depend on the selected model and the content processed.

## Commands for shortcut settings

- `ai/review`
- `ai/proofread`
