# Grouped image masks and folds

[New features](README.md) · [Magyar](../hu/folds.md)

**Where to find it:** Subtitle grid; fold actions in the Subtitle menu.

## Image masks

Grouped for easier selection, deletion, and retiming. Open or close groups with the middle mouse button.

## Folds

Select a complete fold with `Ctrl`; copied folds preserve their state in another subtitle. Open or close with the middle mouse button.

**Timing feedback:** folds and image-mask groups indicate whether they are visible on the current frame.

## Commands for shortcut settings

- `grid/fold/create`
- `grid/fold/toggle`
