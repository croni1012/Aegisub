# Brightness and playback speed

[New features](README.md) · [Magyar](../hu/playback.md)

**Where to find it:** Brightness and speed controls in the video pane.

## Brightness

Lighten or darken the video preview in the 0–400% range.

## Speed

Set playback speed between 0.25× and 10×. Audio follows the video speed. Supported audio players: DirectSound, PulseAudio, and ALSA.

**Quick reset:** right-click either control to restore its default value.
