# Small but useful changes

[New features](README.md) · [Magyar](../hu/other-improvements.md)

**Where to find it:** Timing, video loading, and video preview.

- `video/open/recent` opens the most recent video.
- More reliable loading of MKV files containing over 32 tracks.

- Shift lines to the current frame according to their end times.
- The video does not flicker while resizing.

## Commands for shortcut settings

- `time/frame/current_end`
- `video/open/recent`
